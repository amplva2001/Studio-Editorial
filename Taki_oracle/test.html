T1 · What to say

  1. Step 1: "Imagine you run a small business, like Magnus, an instrument repairer. Before you see any tasks: what does low, medium and high importance mean to you? And when would you want Taki's accountant, a real person, to look at it?" Let them type or tap the suggestions.

T2 · What to say

  1. "Taki will show you some predictions about the business. For each one, choose how you'd want to handle it."

  3. Ask for each: "Why that one?" and "Did the 'how sure' bar change anything?"
  4. At the end: "Which prediction would you never let Taki handle alone?"
